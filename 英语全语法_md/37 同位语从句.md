# 第37章 同位语从句

## 37.1 同位语从句

### 什么是同位语？
我的朋友是一个老师。 My friend is a teacher.
我的朋友，大卫，是一个老师。 My friend, David, is a teacher.
把它放在那儿。 Put it there.
把它放在那儿，桌子上。 Put it there, on the table.
- 前后两者表达内容相同时，后者叫做前者的同位语。

### 同位语从句的引导词
The teacher likes cats. 老师喜欢猫。
The news _____ is unknown. 这个消息是不为人知的。
The news the teacher likes cats is unknown. （错）
The news that the teacher likes cats is unknown.
- that 引导陈述句，无意义，不可省略。

Is she a nurse? 她是个护士吗？
The question _____ is easy. 这个问题很简单。
The question is she a nurse is easy. （错）
The question she is a nurse is easy. （错）
The question whether she is a nurse is easy.

Can you dance? 你会跳舞吗？
The question _____ is easy. 这个问题很简单。
The question can you dance is easy. （错）
The question you can dance is easy. （错）
The question whether you can dance is easy.

Did he sleep? 他睡了吗？
The question _____ is easy. 这个问题很简单。
The question did he sleep is easy. （错）
The question he slept is easy. （错）
The question whether he slept is easy.
- whether 引导一般疑问句，译为"是否"。

What is this? 这是什么？
The question _____ makes me confused.
这个问题使我困惑。
The question what is this makes me confused. （错）
The question what this is makes me confused.

How will you sing? 你将如何唱歌？
The question _____ is important. 这个问题很重要。
The question how will you sing is important. （错）

The question how you will sing is important.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句。

### 同位语从句的语序
- 陈述语序：主语 + 动词
Is he happy? 他高兴吗？
The question _____ is hard. 这个问题很难说。
The question whether he is happy is hard.

Whose elephant is it? 这是谁的大象？
The question _____ is easy. 这个问题很简单。
The question whose elephant it is is easy.

Who bit me just now? 刚才谁咬我？
The question _____ is known to all. 这个问题众所周知。
The question who bit me just now is known to all.

## 37.2 简单原则-同位语从句

### 简单原则-同位语从句
1. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
I know the fact. 我知道这个事实。
He is a teacher. 他是一个老师。
I know the fact he is a teacher.
英语中为了明确主、从句的划分，习惯于给从句加引导词
I know the fact that he is a teacher.
- 于是形成了同位语从句
- that 引导陈述句，无意义。

2. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
He asked a question. 他问了一个问题。
Was she happy? 她开心吗？
He asked a question was she happy. （× 标点冲突）
He asked a question she was happy. （× 句意变了）
- 英语中，从句需要引导词
He asked a question whether she was happy.
- whether 引导一般疑问句。

3. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
He asked a question. 他问了一个问题。
What was it? 那是什么？
He asked a question what was it. （× 标点冲突）
He asked a question what it was.

再看这两句
He asked a question. 他问了一个问题。
Who had a hammer? 谁有锤子？
He asked a question who had a hammer.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句

### 同位语从句如果使用简单原则避免重复会怎样？
I know the fact that you are happy.
- I know that you are happy. 宾语从句
The question is this who ate the bread.
- The question is who ate the bread. 表语从句
The truth that he is a teacher surprised me.
- That he is a teacher surprised me. 主语从句
