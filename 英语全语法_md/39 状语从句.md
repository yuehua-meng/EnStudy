## 39.1 if真实条件状语从句

### 什么是if 真实条件状语从句
if：如果
- 如果假设的条件有可能发生，则为真实条件句。
- 如果假设的条件通常不可能发生，则为虚拟条件句。

如果我饿了，我就会睡觉。
If I am hungry, I will sleep.
如果明天下雨，我就有水喝了。
If it will rain tomorrow, I will have water to drink. （错）
If it rains tomorrow, I will have water to drink.
- if 真实条件句要遵循"主将从现"的原则

3. if 真实条件状语从句与简单句的转换
- 转换为"祈使句，and/or+简单句"
如果你努力学习，你就会通过考试。 If you study hard, you'll pass the exam.
用"祈使句，and+简单句"转换：
努力学习，你就会通过考试。 Study hard, and you'll pass the exam.

如果你不快点，你就会迟到。 If you don't hurry up, you'll be late.
用"祈使句，or+简单句"转换
快点，不然你就会迟到。 Hurry up, or you'll be late.

- 借助介词with 或without 来转换
如果你帮助我，我会很快完成我的工作。 If you help me, I'll finish my job soon.
用with 转换
在你的帮助下，我会很快完成我的工作。 With your help, I'll finish my job soon.

如果没有水，鱼会死的。 If there is no water, fish may die.
用without 转换
没有水鱼会死的。 Fish may die without water.

### 注意
- 在if 真实条件状语从句中，当主句是一般将来时时，不使用be going to do 的结构。
如果明天我有空，我会帮你学英语。
I'll help you with your English if I am free tomorrow.

## 39.2 状语从句1

### 什么是状语从句？
- 状语表达如下内容
时间、地点、原因、结果、目的、让步、比较、方式、伴随、程度、条件、频率。
I always get up early to make lunch. 我总是早起做午餐。
He never runs with a dog at home. 他从不在家和狗跑步。
- 状语由句子充当时，就是状语从句。

### 时间状语从句
引导词：when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as, the day, the moment, the minute,
the second, every time, each time 等。
当我到家时，我就给你打电话。 When I get home, I will call you.
直到我成为了一个父亲我才意识到这个。 I didn't realize it until I became a father.

### 地点状语从句
引导词：where, wherever, anywhere, everywhere 等。
有工厂的地方空气污染就严重。 Air is heavily polluted where there are factories.
你应该把书放回原来的地方。 You should put the book where it was.

### 原因状语从句
引导词：because, since, as, now that 等。
他们不喜欢我，因为我长得丑。 They don't like me because I am ugly.

### 目的状语从句
引导词：so that, in order that, in case 等。
他提高了声音，以防我们听不到。 He raised his voice in case we couldn't hear him.
我吃很多食物以便于能够长胖。 I eat a lot of food so that I can get fat.

### 结果状语从句
引导词：so…that…, such…that… 等。
他很早起床以至于赶上了第一班公共汽车。 He got up so early that he caught the first bus.
这是一个好机会，千万不能错过它。 It's such a good chance that we mustn't miss it.

### 条件状语从句
引导词：if, unless, in case, as long as 等。
如果他同意，我们将开始我们的项目。 We'll start our project if he agrees.
你会成功，只要你努力工作。 You will succeed as long as you work hard.

### 让步状语从句
引导词：though, although, even if, even though, whatever, whoever, wherever, whenever,
however, whichever,
"no matter + 特殊疑问词"等。
虽然我很累，但依旧想工作。 Though I am tired, I still want to work.

无论你去哪儿，我都跟着你。
Wherever you go, I will follow you.
No matter where you go, I will follow you.

### 比较状语从句
引导词：than, as 等。
他的无礼已经超过了我所能忍受的限度。 His rudeness is more than I can bear.
老师像我一样高。 The teacher is as tall as I am.

### 方式状语从句
引导词：as, as if, the way 等。
入乡随俗。 When in Rome, do as the Romans do.

## 39.3 状语从句2

### 状语从句主语的省略
- 当状语从句的主语是虚主语it，或者状语从句的主语和主句主语相同时，则从句可省略。
其方法是：
1. 先把从句的主语和be 动词去掉。
2. 然后把剩余动词变为分词形式：主动关系用现在分词，被动关系用过去分词。
If it is possible, you can come to my house.
If possible, you can come to my house.

When she was cooking, she heard someone call.
When cooking, she heard someone call.

I was surprised when I came into the classroom.
I was surprised when coming into the classroom.

The elephant is happy, though it is left in the zoo.
The elephant is happy, though left in the zoo.

Because he is to work, he must get up early.
Because to work, he must get up early.

Before I sleep, I will finish my homework.
Before sleeping, I will finish my homework.

After he was taken home, the boy cried.
After taken home, the boy cried. （错）
After being taken home, the boy cried.
- before/after 加句子时是连词，不加句子是介词。

### 状语从句引导词的省略
- 状语从句主语省略之后，在不影响句意的情况下，引导词也可以省略。
When I sang, I felt happy.
When singing, I felt happy.
Singing, I felt happy.
- 现在分词作状语。

After I ate dinner, I went for a walk.
After eating dinner, I went for a walk.
Eating dinner, I went for a walk. （错）
Having eaten dinner, I went for a walk.

Though it is left in the zoo, the elephant is happy.
Though left in the zoo, the elephant is happy.

Left in the zoo, the elephant is happy.
- 过去分词作状语。

Because he is to work, he must get up early.
Because to work, he must get up early.
To work, he must get up early.
- 动词不定式作状语。

When he was at school, he studied English.
When at school, he studied English.
At school, he studied English.
- 介词短语作状语。

When I am happy, I will sing.
When happy, I will sing.
Happy, I will sing.
- 形容词作状语。

- 回想一个问题：状语从句省略的第一步是什么？
先把从句主语和be 动词省略。
- 倘若只省略主语，不省略be 动词会怎样？
When he was at school, he studied English.
When was at school, he studied English. （错）
When being at school, he studied English.
Being at school, he studied English.

## 39.4 简单原则-状语从句

### 简单原则-状语从句
- 状语从句的形成
I am hungry. 我饿了。
I will have dinner. 我要去吃饭。
If I am hungry, I will have dinner.
When I am hungry, I will have dinner.
Because I am hungry, I will have dinner.

When I was singing, I felt happy.
当我唱歌的时候，我感到开心。
1. 省略主语：When singing, I felt happy.
2. 省略引导词：Singing, I felt happy.
- 现在分词作状语

Because I was fired, I felt sad.
因为我被解雇了，我感到伤心。
1. 省略主语：Because fired, I felt sad.
2. 省略引导词：Fired, I felt sad.
- 过去分词作状语

Because I am to sing, I feel happy.
因为我要去唱歌，所以我感到开心。
1. 省略主语：Because to sing, I feel happy.
2. 省略引导词：To sing, I feel happy.
- 动词不定式作状语

When I am happy, I will dance.
当我开心的时候，我会跳舞。
1. 省略主语：When happy, I will dance.
2. 省略引导词：Happy, I will dance.
- 形容词作状语

When I am in the zoo, I will feel happy.
当我在动物园的时候，我会感到开心。
1. 省略主语：When in the zoo, I will feel happy.
2. 省略引导词：In the zoo, I will feel happy.
