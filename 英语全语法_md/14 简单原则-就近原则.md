# 第14章 简单原则-就近原则

## 14 简单原则-就近原则

### 简单原则-就近原则
在 there be 句型中，我们学过这样的句子：
There is an apple and five oranges. 有一个苹果和五个橙子。
- 为什么be 动词使用了is？
就近原则
- 真的有就近原则吗？
There is an apple. 有一个苹果。
There are five oranges. 有五个橙子。
- 两句话加一起会的到什么？
There is an apple and there are five oranges.
There is an apple and five oranges.
- 简单原则
1. Neither you are happy, nor he is happy. 你也不开心，他也不开心。
Neither you are happy, nor he.
Neither you nor he is happy.
2. Either you are happy, or he is happy. 要么你开心，要么他开心。
Either you or he is happy.
3. Not only you are happy, but also he is happy. 不但你开心，而且他开心。
Not only you but also he is happy.
