# 第13章 there be句型

## 13 there be句型

### "有"是什么意思
1. 我有一个尾巴。
有：拥有，表示所属关系，对应的单词是"have"。
I have a tail.
2. 书包里有一头大象。
有：存在，对应的是"there be"。
There is an elephant in the schoolbag.

### There be 句型的结构
- There is + 单数名词/不可数名词 + 地点。
教室里有一个老师。There is a teacher in the classroom.
杯子里有一些空气。There is some air in the glass.
- There are + 复数名词 + 地点。
课桌上有五台电脑。There are five computers on the desk.
头上有一只小鸟和两只老鹰。
There are a bird and two eagles on my head. （错）
There is a bird and two eagles on my head.

### There be 句型的就近原则
- There be 句型be 动词的选择取决于离它最近的主语。
口袋里有一支钢笔和两个鸡蛋。
There is a pen and two eggs in the pocket.
There are two eggs and a pen in the pocket.
床上有一篮子土豆。 There is a basket of potatoes on the bed.
桌子下面有两瓶牛奶。 There are two bottles of milk under the table.
房间里有很多老师。 There are a lot of teachers in the room.
- There be 句型为什么会存在就近原则？
桌上有一个苹果。 There is an apple on the table.
桌上有两个橙子。 There are two oranges on the table.
两句话合并会导致两个there be 重复，所以省略一个：
There is an apple on the table and there are two oranges on the table.
There is an apple and two oranges on the table.

### There be 句型的句型转换
1. 杯子里有一些牛奶。
There is some milk in the glass.
否定句：There isn't any milk in the glass.
一般疑问句：Is there any milk in the glass?
Yes, there is.
No, there isn't.
2. 书桌上有十本书。
There are ten books on the desk.

对 on the desk 提问：Where are ten books?
对 ten books 提问：What is on the desk?
对 ten 提问：How many books are there on the desk?
3. 瓶子里有两吨水。
There is two tons of water in the bottle.
对 two tons 提问：How much water is there in the bottle?

### There be 句型的扩展
- There is/are + n. + doing sth. + 地点
马桶里有一只小鱼在游泳。There's a fish swimming in the toilet.
床下有一些孩子在唱歌。There're some children singing under the bed.
