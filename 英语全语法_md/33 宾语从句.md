# 第33章 宾语从句

## 33.1 宾语从句1

### 先造几个句子
我认为大象跑的很快。 I think the elephant runs fast.

### 什么是宾语从句？
当宾语由一个句子充当时，这个句子就是宾语从句。

### 宾语从句的引导词
He is a teacher.
I know _____.
I know he is a teacher.
I know (that) he is a teacher.
- that 引导陈述句，无意义，可省略。

Can he swim?
I wonder _____.
I wonder he can swim. （错）
I wonder if/whether he can swim.

Is she a student?
I wonder _____.
I wonder she is a student. （错）
I wonder if/whether she is a student.

Do they speak English?
I wonder _____.
I wonder they speak English. （错）
I wonder if/whether they speak English.
- if/whether 引导一般疑问句，译为"是否"。区别是：
1. 当宾语从句置于句首，只用 whether。如：
这是否真实，我不知道。 Whether this is true, I don't know.
注意：以上这种语序是为了强调宾语从句。
2. 当宾语从句跟在介词后，只用 whether。如：
这要看我们是否有足够的钱。 It depends on whether we have enough money.
3. 不能用 if or not, 但可以用 whether or not, whether…or not 或 if…or not。如：
我想知道你会不会游泳。
I want to know if you can swim or not.
I want to know whether you can swim or not.
I want to know whether or not you can swim.
4. 不能用 if to do sth., 但可以用 whether to do sth.（此时已不再是从句）。如：
我决定不了今天要不要做作业。 I can't decide whether to do my homework today.
5. if 既可以翻译成"是否"，又可以翻译成"如果" 。当 if 会引起歧义时，只用 whether。如：
你能告诉我是否知道答案吗？

Could you tell me if you know the answer? （错）
Could you tell me whether you know the answer?

What is his name?
I wonder _____.
I wonder what his name is.

When can they come back?
I wonder _____.
I wonder when they can come back.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句。

What a dirty book!
He says _____.
He says what a dirty book.

How happy he is!
He says _____.
He says how happy he is!
- what/how 引导感叹句。

Open the door.
He says _____.  （无法放入）
He asks me to open the door.
- ask/tell/order sb. to do sth. 转述祈使句。

### 宾语从句的语序
- 陈述语序：主语 + 动词
Will he be a policeman?
I wonder _____.
I wonder if he will be a policeman.

Where can we have a picnic?
I wonder _____.
I wonder where we can have a picnic.

What ate that banana?
I wonder _____.
I wonder what ate that banana.

### 宾语从句的时态
我认为这道题很简单。 I think that the question is very easy.
我认为他昨天很高兴。 I think that he was happy yesterday.
我认为他明天会睡觉。 I think that he will sleep tomorrow.

1. 当主句是现在时，从句可用各种时态。
我曾以为他一个人生活。 I thought he lived alone.
我曾以为他会去看电影。 I thought he would go to the cinema.
我曾以为他吃过晚饭了。 I thought he had eaten dinner.
2. 当主句是过去时，从句可用各种过去时态。
3. 客观真理任何时候都用一般现在时。
他曾告诉我们光速比声速快。 He told us light travels faster than sound.

## 33.2 宾语从句2

### 宾语从句的否定转移
- 当主语是第一人称，且动词是表示主语主观想法的词时，其否定形式要用在主句上。
我认为你不喜欢大象。
I think you don't like elephants. （错）
I don't think you like elephants.

我知道你不是老师。 I know you are not a teacher.
他认为你不是老师。 He thinks you are not a teacher.

### 形式宾语
我觉得我们每天都做作业很有趣。
I find that we do homework every day funny.
I find it funny that we do homework every day.

我认为人们应该早起是这儿的一条规定。
I think that people should get up early a rule here.
I think it a rule here that people should get up early.

我认为我总是睡觉这事太可怕了。
I think that I always sleep terrible.
I think it terrible that I always sleep.
I think it is terrible that I always sleep.
I think that I always sleep is terrible.

8. 宾语从句的缩略形式
他在想他是否应该帮助我们。
He is thinking whether he should help us. = He is thinking whether to help us.

我不知道我该做什么。
I don't know what I can do. = I don't know what to do.

他告诉我我可以去哪里？
He told me where I could go. = He told me where to go.

- 当宾语从句的主语在主句中出现过，此时，宾语从句可缩写为：引导词 + to do （that/if
除外）
He told me where I could go. = He told me where to go.
- 这样的句子要结合上下文理解句意。

## 33.3 简单原则-宾语从句

### 简单原则-宾语从句
1. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
I know it. 我知道这件事。
He is a teacher. 他是一个老师。
I know it he is a teacher.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复：I know he is a teacher.
- 于是形成了宾语从句
英语中为了明确主、从句的划分，习惯于给从句加引导词
I know that he is a teacher.
- that 引导陈述句，无意义，可省略。

2. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
He asked a question. 他问了一个问题。
Was she happy? 她开心吗？
He asked a question was she happy.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
He asked was she happy. （×  标点冲突）
He asked she was happy. （×  句意变了）
- 英语中，从句需要引导词
He asked if/whether she was happy.
- if/whether 引导一般疑问句。

3. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
He asked a question. 他问了一个问题。
What was it? 那是什么？
He asked a question what was it.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
He asked what was it. （×  标点冲突）
He asked what it was.

再看这两句
He asked a question. 他问了一个问题。
Who had a hammer? 谁有锤子？
He asked a question who had a hammer.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复：He asked who had a hammer.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句
