# 第47章 it的用法

## 47 it的用法

### 人称代词 it
- 人称代词 it 用来指代不是人的"它"。
我有一只狗。它很大。 I have a dog. It is very big.
这朵花很漂亮，但闻起来很糟糕。 The flower is beautiful, but it smells terrible.

### 指示代词 it
- 如果 it 指的不是非人的"它"，那便是指示代词it。
是谁呀？（有人敲门时） Who is it?
我们必须喝水。这很重要。 We must drink water. It is important.

### 虚主语 it
- 虚主语it 通常用来表示时间、距离、价值、天气、温度、情况等等。
现在5 点了。 It's five o'clock now.
今天很热。 It's hot today.
太远了。 It's too far.
太可怕了。 It's horrible.

### 形式主语 it
- 若主语太长，则可以将主语放于句尾，开头用 it 作形式主语。
To finish the work is too difficult.
is too difficult to finish the work. （错）
It is too difficult to finish the work.

That I must study hard is known to all.
is known to all that I must study hard. （错）
It is known to all that I must study hard.

### 形式宾语 it
- 若宾语太长，则可以将宾语放于句尾，原位置用 it 作形式宾语
I find eating with hands fun.
I find fun eating with hands. （错）
I find it fun eating with hands.

I think that we get up early useful.
I think useful that we get up early. （错）
I think it useful that we get up early.

### 强调句
强调句是一个死板的句型，其结构是：
It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 剩余部分。
例：I have dinner at home in the evening.

- 强调 I
It is I that/who have dinner at home in the evening.
- 强调 dinner
It is dinner that I have at home in the evening.
- 强调 at home
It is at home that I have dinner in the evening.
- 强调 in the evening
It is in the evening that I have dinner at home.
