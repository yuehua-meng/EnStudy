# 第32章 引语与过去将来时

## 32.1 引语与过去将来时1

### 什么是引语
引用别人的语言叫引语。

### 如何引用别人的语言
I am an elephant.
直接引用
He says, "I am an elephant."
间接引用
He says he is an elephant.

### 直接引语转间接引语
1. 人称变化
She says, "I am happy."
She says she is happy.

She tells me, "You are great."
She tells me I am great.

She tells me, "He is clever."
She tells me he is clever.
- 规则：一主二宾三不变

2. 引导词
She says, "He is handsome.”
She says he is handsome.
She says (that) he is handsome.
- that 引导陈述句，无意义，可省略。

She asks, "Is he handsome?”
She asks is he handsome. （错）
She asks if/whether he is handsome.
- if/whether 引导一般疑问句等，译为是否。

She asks, "What is his name?”
She asks what is his name. （错）
She asks what his name is.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句。

She exclaims, "How clever he is!"
She exclaims how clever he is.
- what/how 引导感叹句。

She says to me, "Open the door."
She says to me open the door. （错）
She asks me to open the door.
- ask/tell/order sb. to do sth. 转述祈使句。

3. 语序
- 陈述语序：主语 + 动词
He asks, "Can she swim?"
He asks if she can swim.

He asks, "When will she get up?"
He asks when she will get up.

He asks, "When does she get up?"
He asks when she gets up.

He asks, "Who ate that apple?"
He asks who ate that apple.

## 32.2 引语与过去将来时2

### 引语转换的时态问题
- 用现在时转述，时态不变。用过去时转述，时态倒退。
"I am happy.”
He says he is happy.
He said he was happy.

"I was happy."
He says he was happy.
He said he had been happy.

"I have eaten dinner."
He says he has eaten dinner.
He said he had eaten dinner.

"I had seen it before lunch."
He says he had seen it before lunch.
He said he had seen it before lunch.

"I am eating an egg."
He says he is eating an egg.
He said he was eating an egg.

### 过去将来时的产生

"I will eat an egg."
He says he will eat an egg.
He said he would eat an egg.
- 一般过去将来时：would do

"I will be eating an egg."
He says he will be eating an egg.
He said he would be eating an egg.
- 过去将来进行时：would be doing

"I will have eaten an egg."
He says he will have eaten an egg.
He said he would have eaten an egg.
- 过去将来完成时：would have done

"I will have been eating an egg."
He says he will have been eating an egg.
He said he would have been eating an egg.
- 过去将来完成进行时：would have been
doing

- 时态难点
He said, "The earth goes around the sun."
He said the earth goes around the sun.

He said, "I am a student."
He said he was a student.
He said he is a student.

### 引语转换的逻辑问题
- 逻辑变化
用过去时转述的时候，还会存在逻辑变化。
He said, "I am happy today."
He said he was happy that day.

He said, "This elephant is beautiful."
He said that elephant was beautiful.

He said, "She will come to my office tomorrow."
He said she would go to his office the next day.

- 逻辑变化总结

| 类别 | 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间状语 | now | then |
|  | ago | before |
|  | today | that day |
|  | yesterday | the day before |
|  | tomorrow | the next day |
|  | last week | the week before |
|  | next week | the next week |
| 指示代词 | this | that |
|  | these | those |
| 地点状语 | here | there |
| 方向动词 | come | go |
