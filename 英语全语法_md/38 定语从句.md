# 第38章 定语从句

## 38.1 定语从句1

### 定语从句的由来
I know the boy. 我认识这个男孩。
The boy is standing at the door. 这个男孩站在门边。
I know the boy the boy is standing at the door. （错）
I know the boy who is standing at the door.
- who 不翻译成"谁"，此处用来指代the boy。

I lost the book. 我把这本书弄丢了
I bought the book yesterday. 我昨天买的这本书。
I lost the book I bought the book yesterday.  （错）
I lost the book which I bought yesterday.
- which 不翻译成"哪一个"，此处用来指代the book。

### 什么是定语从句
定语：起到修饰、限定作用的成分。
定语从句：起到修饰、限定作用的句子。
I know the boy who is standing at the door.
先行词：被修饰的词
关系词： 定语从句的引导词
- 关系代词
1. 先行词是人：who, (whom), that
他就是想见你的人吗？ Is he the man who/that wants to see you?
他就是我曾看见的那个人。 He is the man who/whom/that I saw.
2. 先行词非人：which, that
我买了些三条腿的桌子。 I bought some desks which/that have three legs.
我有你喜欢的那本书。 I have the book which/that you like.
3. whose 指代先行词的所有格
我认识那个帽子是绿色的老师。 I know that teacher whose hat is green.

### 关系代词的在定语从句中的成分
Is he the man who/that wants to see you?
I bought some desks which/that have three legs.
- 关系代词在定语从句中作主语

He is the man who/whom/that I saw.
I have the book which/that you like.
- 关系代词在定语从句中作宾语/表语。

### 定语从句的省略
1. 关系代词作在定语从句中作宾语/表语时，可直接省略。
He is the man who/whom/that I saw.
省略为：He is the man I saw.

I have the book which/that you like.
省略为：I have the book you like.

2. 关系代词作主语时，若省略，首先将关系代词和be 动词一起省略，再将剩余动词变为分
词形式。主动关系，用现在分词；被动关系，用过去分词。
我认识那个正站在门边的男孩。
I know the boy who is standing at the door.
I know the boy standing at the door.
- 现在分词作定语

我知道那条横穿公园的小河。
I know the river that cuts across the park.
I know the river cutting across the park.
- 现在分词作定语

开车的男士没有看见她。
The man who drove the car didn't see her.
The man driving the car didn't see her.
- 现在分词作定语

森林里住着很多动物。
There are a lot of animals which live in the forest.
There are a lot of animals living in the forest.
- There be + 主语 + doing sth. + 地点。

被带到动物园的这只小猫很可爱。
The cat which was taken to the zoo is cute.
The cat taken to the zoo is cute.
- 过去分词作定语

我买了一个浇花的东西。
I bought something that is to water flowers.
I bought something to water flowers.
- 动词不定式作定语

我买了一个有用的东西。
I bought something that is useful.
I bought something useful.
- 形容词作后置定语

包里的大象很漂亮。
The elephant which is in the bag is beautiful.
The elephant in the bag is beautiful.
- 介词短语作定语

## 38.2 定语从句2

### 关系代词的区别
1. 当先行词是序数词，或被序数词、最高级、the only, the very, the same, the last, all, any
no, much, little, few, every 等词修饰时，关系代词常用that。
她是唯一的一个懂我的老师。 She is the only teacher that understands me.
2. 当关系代词作表语时，常用that。
他已经不是过去的那个人了。 He is no longer the man that he used to be.
3. 当先行词既有人又有非人时，关系代词用that。
他称赞了他所看到的这些孩子们以及他们的作业。
He praised the children and their homework that he saw.
4. 当先行词是something, anything, nothing, none, few, little, much, a lot, all, any, those,
everyone, everybody, anyone, anybody, someone, somebody 等词时，关系代词指人常用
who；指非人常用that。
有人会游泳吗？ Is there anyone who can swim?
所有能做的都已经做了。 All that can be done has been done.
5. 当关系代词位于介词的后面时：指人用whom，指非人用which。
我给买了个娃娃的那个女孩是我的妹妹。 The girl for whom I bought a doll is my sister.
我看见了那个上面有苹果的桌子。 I saw the table on which there was an apple?
6. 非限定性定语从句中，关系代词不用that。
我认识这个女孩，她住在北京。 I know the girl, who lives in Beijing.
我喜欢苹果，苹果长在树上。 I like apples, which grow on a tree.
7. 关系代词要注意避免重复、混淆。
哪辆车撞着这颗树了？ Which is the car that hit the tree?
在那跑步的人是谁？ Who is the man that is running over there?
他想买那些卖得最好的书。 He wants to buy those books which sell best.
她是我认识的那个会跳舞的女孩。 She is the girl I know who can dance.

## 38.3 定语从句3

### 定语从句的引导词
This is the classroom. 这就是那个教室。
We study in the classroom. 我们在那个教室学习。
This is the classroom we study in the classroom. （错）
This is the classroom which/that we study in.
This is the classroom in which we study.

I remember that moment. 我记得那一刻。
I was running at that moment. 那一刻我正在跑步。
I remember that moment I was running at that moment. （错）
I remember that moment which/that I was running at.
I remember that moment at which I was running.

This is the reason. 这就是原因。
He is late for the reason. 他由于这个原因迟到了。
This is the reason he is late for the reason. （错）
This is the reason which/that he is late for.
This is the reason for which he is late.

He is my father. 他是我的爸爸。
I bought a dress for my father. 我为我爸买了一条裙子。
He is my father I bought a dress for my father. （错）
He is my father who/whom/that I bought a dress for.
He is my father for whom I bought a dress.

This is the classroom in which we study.
I remember that moment at which I was running.
This is the reason for which he is late.
He is my father for whom I bought a dress.
- "介词+which/whom"引导定语从句。
- 引导定语从句时，介词后不用that/who。

This is the classroom in which we study.
I remember that moment at which I was running.
This is the reason for which he is late.
问题：以上这种引导方式可否简化？
- in which 表示地点，可以用where 代替：
This is the classroom where we study.
- at which 表示时间，可以用when 代替：
I remember that moment when I was running.
- for which 表示原因，可以用why 代替：
This is the reason why he is late.

### 关系副词引导的定语从句
- where 表示地点
- when 表示时间
- why 表示原因

- way 后面的定语从句用什么引导词？
这就是我走路的方式。
This is the way in which I walk.
This is the way that I walk.
This is the way I walk.

### 关系副词的省略
1. the place, somewhere, anywhere, everywhere, nowhere 等表示地点的词后的关系副词
where 有时可以省略。
This is the place (where) we met years ago.  这就是多年前我们见面的地方。
2. the time, every time, each time, the moment 等表示时间的词后的关系副词when 可以省
略。
I remember the day (when) we met for the first time. 我记得我们第一次相遇的那一天。
3. 先行词the reason 后面的关系副词why 可以省略。
That's the reason (why) I asked you to come. 这就是我叫你来的原因。

### 非限定性定语从句
- 对比两句话
The boy who is running is my brother. 正在跑步的那个男孩是我哥哥。
The young man is a teacher, who likes eating shoes. 这个年轻人是个老师，他喜欢吃鞋子。

- 对先行词限定性不强，用逗号隔开的定语从句，就是非限定性定语从句。
我的房间，一个漂亮的地方，是明亮的。 My room, which is a beautiful place, is bright.
- 非限定性定语从句的引导词不用that。

彼得现在在巴黎了，你在伦敦见过他。 Peter, who/whom you met in London, is now in Paris.
- 非限定性定语从句的引导词不用that。

这有一个公园，人们常在此散步。
Here is a park, where people often take a walk.
Here is a park, in which people often take a walk.

他5 月1 日来，那时他有空。
He'll come on May 1st, when he'll be free.
He'll come on May 1st, on which he'll be free.

我知道你的理由，由于这个理由你没有来。
I know your reason, for which you didn't come.
- 非限定性定语从句中，for which 不用why 代替。

他不爱学习，这使我生气。
He doesn't like study, which makes me angry.
He doesn't like study, as makes me angry.
- which 和as 可以指代前面的一句话。

他不相信我的话，这是我知道的。
He doesn't believe me, as I know.
As I know, he doesn't believe me.
- as 引导定语从句，可放在句首，常译为"正如"。

## 38.4 简单原则-定语从句

### 简单原则-定语从句
I like the book. 我喜欢那本书。
He bought the book. 他买了那本书。
I like the book he bought the book.
简单原则，避免重复：
I like the book he bought.
我喜欢他买的那本书。
- 省略引导词的定语从句

I like the book he bought the book.
I like the book he bought which/that. （错）
I like the book which/that he bought.
- 由关系代词which/that 引导的定语从句

I know the boy. 我认识这个男孩。
The boy likes apples. 这个男孩喜欢苹果。
I know the boy the boy likes apples.
I know the boy who/that likes apples.
- 由关系代词who/that 引导的定语从句

I know the boy. 我认识这个男孩。
She met the boy. 她遇到了那个男孩。
I know the boy she met the boy.
I know the boy she met who/whom/that. （错）
I know the boy who/whom/that she met.
- 由关系代词who/whom/that 引导的定语从句

I like the room. 我喜欢这个房间。
You dance in the room. 你在这个房间跳舞。
I like the room you dance in the room.
I like the room you dance in which. （错）
I like the room in which you dance.
I like the room where you dance.
- 由"介词+which"或关系副词where 引导定语从句

I remember the day. 我记得那天。
He went away on that day. 那天他离开了。
I remember the day he went away on that day.
I remember the day he went away on which. （错）
I remember the day on which he went away.
I remember the day when he went away.

- 由"介词+which"或关系副词when 引导定语从句

Tell me the reason. 把原因告诉我。
You are late for the reason. 由于这个原因你迟到了。
Tell me the reason you are late for the reason.
Tell me the reason you are late for which. （错）
Tell me the reason for which you are late.
Tell me the reason why you are late.
- 由"介词+which"或关系副词why 引导定语从句

I know the girl. 我认识那个女孩。
The girl is singing. 那个女孩正在唱歌。
I know the girl the girl is singing.
I know the girl singing. 我认识那个正在唱歌的女孩。
- 定语从句省略：现在分词作定语

I like the book. 我喜欢那本书。
The book was bought by him. 这本书是他买的。
I like the book the book was bought by him.
I like the book bought by him. 我喜欢他买的那本书。
- 定语从句省略：过去分词作定语

I got something. 我得到一些东西。
It is to clean the room. 它是用来打扫房间的。
I got something it is to clean the room.
I got something to clean the room.
我得到一些打扫房间的东西。
- 定语从句省略：动词不定式作定语

I got something. 我得到一些东西。
It is valuable. 它是有价值的。
I got something it is valuable.
I got something valuable.
- 定语从句省略：形容词作定语

I can see the cat. 我能看见这只猫。
The cat is on the table. 猫在桌子上。
I can see the cat the cat is on the table.
I can see the cat on the table.
- 定语从句省略：介词短语作定语
