# 第45章 虚拟语气

## 45.1 虚拟语气1

### 什么是虚拟语气
英语中有三种语气
1. 陈述语气 (the indicative mood)
in- 进入；dic - 说；
2. 祈使语气 (the imperative mood)
-imper- 统治
3. 虚拟语气 (the subjunctive mood)
-sub- 在下面；-junct- 加入、连接

- 虚拟语气的本质含义
放低自己的心态，以低姿态参与到事情中的语言表达方式。
- 什么时候要放低姿态？
1. 不可能的假想。
2. 等等。

### 不可能的假想
表达与事实相反的内容
- 核心原则：姿态放低、时态降低

### if 虚拟条件句

if 从句

主句

与现在相反

did/were

would do

与过去相反

had done

would have done

与将来相反

did/were
were to do
should do

would do

1. 与现在相反
如果我是鸟，我会在空中翱翔。
If I were a bird, I would fly in the sky.
2. 与过去相反
如果医生早点到，他会得救的。
If the doctor had got here earlier, he would have been saved.
3. 与将来相反
如果他明天来的话，我就跟他谈谈。
If he came tomorrow, I would talk to him.
If he were to come tomorrow, I would talk to him.
If he should come tomorrow, I would talk to him.
4. 错综条件句
如果我在学校学习刻苦的话，我现在也会成为一个工程师了。
If I had worked hard at school, I would be an engineer.

### if 虚拟条件句的倒装
- if 虚拟条件句的倒装：当if 从句的动词中含有were, should, had 时，可if 可以省略，把
were, should, had 等词置于句首，形成倒装语序。
要是他答应的话，我们就派他去。
If he should agree, we would send him there.
倒装：Should he agree, we would send him there.

如果她在这儿的话，她会同意我们的。
If she were here, she would agree with us.
倒装：Were she here, she would agree with us.

如果他学过英语的话，我们已经聘用他了。
If he had learnt English, we would have hired him.
倒装：Had he learnt English, we would have hired him.

- if 虚拟条件句的倒装：若if 虚拟条件句是否定句，否定词not 应置于主语之后，不能与
were, should, had 等缩略成weren't, shouldn't, hadn't 而置于句首。
如果他不是个老师，他就走了。
If he weren't a teacher, he would leave.
倒装：Were he not a teacher, he would leave.

## 45.2 虚拟语气2

### 不可能的假想

### if 虚拟条件句的省略：含蓄条件句
- 用without, but for, but that 等引导虚拟条件
要不是他的帮助，我们还会在工作呢。
If it were without his help, we would be working now.
If without his help, we would be working now.
Without his help, we would be working now.

If it weren't for his help, we would be working now.
If not for his help, we would be working now.
But for his help, we would be working now.

要不是我亲眼目睹，我还不信呢。
But that I saw it, I wouldn't have believed it.

- 动词不定式引导含蓄条件
如果能与你一起去，我将很高兴。
If I were to go with you, I should be happy.
If to go with you, I should be happy.
I should be happy to go with you.

- 现在分词引导含蓄条件
假若这次失败，你会受到惩罚。
If you failed this time, you would be punished.
If failing this time, you would be punished.
Failing this time, you would be punished.

- 过去分词引导含蓄条件
如果他被送去上学，他会成为一个老师。
If he were sent to school, he would be a teacher.
If sent to school, he would be a teacher.
Sent to school, he would be a teacher.

### even if / even though 引导让步状语从句，表达与事实相反的内容。其动词形式与if 虚
拟条件句相同。
即使他在这儿，也不知该怎么办。
Even though he were here, he wouldn't know what to do.
即使华佗来了也救不了他。
Nobody could save him even if Hua Tuo came.
- 对比以下翻译
即使你不成功，他们也会爱你。
They would also love you even if you didn't succeed.

They will also love you even if you don't succeed.

### wish 表示不可能发生的愿望。姿态放低，时态降低。
我希望我有你那样的头脑。
I wish I had your brains.
我希望我那时就知道真相。
I wish I had known the truth.
我希望我还能再有一次机会。
I wish I would have a chance again.
- 对比
我希望明天能出发。
I wish I could go tomorrow.
I wish I can go tomorrow.

### if only 表示不可能发生的愿望。姿态放低，时态降低。
但愿我不这样紧张。 If only I weren't so nervous.
要是昨晚我没有到那里去就好了。 If only I hadn't gone there last night.
要是能跟他一起走就好了。 If only I could go with him.

### would rather 表示不可能发生的愿望。姿态放低, 时态降低。要注意用过去时表示将来。
我倒想你现在在这儿。 I'd rather you were here now.
我宁愿你昨天看过了这场电影。 I'd rather you had seen the film yesterday.
我们宁愿你明天去那儿。 We'd rather you went there tomorrow.

### as if/as though 引导状语从句，表达与事实相反的内容。姿态放低，时态降低。
他表现的好像很年轻似的。 He acts as if he were young.
我感觉我们好像是朋友好多年了。 I felt as though we had been friends for years.
看起来好像要下雨。 It looks as if it might rain.
- 对比
看样子他好像很年轻。
He looks as if he were young.
He looks as if he is young.

### It is (high/about) time + that 从句表示"早该…"
- that 从句动词用did
我该去接我的儿子了。 It is (high/about) time that I went to pick up my son.
- that 从句动词用 should do (不常用，且此时should 不可省略)
你早该上班了。 It is (high/about) time that you should go to work.

## 45.3 虚拟语气3

### 低调的高姿态：should
1. 表示命令、建议、要求的动词，其后面的从句动词要使用should do 的形式，should 可
省略。
- insist（坚持要求）后的宾语从句
他坚持要我看他的信。 He insisted that I (should) read his letter.
对比：他坚持说我看过他的信。 He insisted that I had read his letter.
- advise, suggest, propose, recommend（建议）后的宾语从句
他建议我们待在这儿。 He suggested that we (should) stay here.
对比：证据都表明是他偷了钱。 The evidence suggests that he stole the money.
- order, command（命令）后的宾语从句
他命令把它送回去。 He ordered that it (should) be sent back.
- ask, demand, require, request, desire（要求）后的宾语从句
他要求我出场。 He requires that I (should) appear.
- move, vote（提议、投票）后的宾语从句
我提议通过这项提案。 I move that we (should) accept the proposal.
- urge（敦促）后的宾语从句
他敦促他们到欧洲去。 He urged that they (should) go to Europe.
- arrange（安排）后的宾语从句
他安排我去国外。 He arranged that I (should) go abroad.
- intend（希望、打算）后的宾语从句
他打算封锁这条消息。 He intended that the news (should) be suppressed.
- direct（指示）后的宾语从句
他指示释放那些俘虏。 He directed that the prisoners (should) be set free.

2. 表示命令、建议、要求的名词后，表语从句或同位语从句要用should do 的形式，should
可省略。
包括：advice, agreement, command, decision, decree, demand, determination, indication,
insistence, order, preference, proposal, request, requirement, stipulation, suggestion, idea,
plan, order 等名词。
我们的建议是你应该第一个去。 Our suggestion is that you (should) be the first to go.

3. It 作形式主语时，下列单词后的主语从句要用 should do 或 should have done，should
视情况省略。
- It's + (important, necessary, strange, advisable, vital, natural, anxious, compulsory, crucial,
desirable, eager, fitting, imperative, essential, impossible, obligatory, urgent, possible,
preferable, probable, improper 等) + that. . .
奇怪的是他竟然是我的朋友。 It is strange that he should be my friend.
你现在就做决定是至关重要的。 It's important that you (should) make a decision now.
对比：你昨天做了决定，这非常重要。 It's important that you made a decision yesterday.
- It is/was desired (suggested, proposed, requested, settled, decided, recommended 等)
that...
建议该投票了。 It is requested that a vote (should) be taken.

- It's a pity that…
真遗憾他竟会干出这种事。 It is a pity that he should have done it.

4. be + amazed, angry, annoyed, astonished, frightened, disappointed, happy, pleased,
proud, sorry, surprised, upset 等词后面的宾语从句要用 should do 或 should have done，
此时 should 表示"竟然"，不可省略。
他很生气，你竟然对他直呼其名。 He was angry that you should call him by name.
我很遗憾，你这次考试竟然失败了。  I'm sorry that you should have failed the exam.

5. 在for fear that（生怕，唯恐），in case（以防），lest（以免，免得）引导的状语从句中，
动词用 should do 的形式，其中 should 可省略。should 在这里表示可能性。
请你明天提醒我，以免我忘记。 Please remind me tomorrow in case I (should) forget.

6. though/although 引导的让步状语从句中，动词用should do 的形式，其中should 可省
略。这种让步并不是事实，在这里should 表示可能性。
尽管他可能会迟到，他还是个好学生。 Although he (should) be late, he is a good student.
虽然他可能会责备我，我仍然会信任他。 Though he (should) blame me, I'll still trust him.

7. whether…or…引导的让步状语从句中，动词用should do 的形式，其中should 可省略。
should 在这里表示可能性。
无论疾病还是健康，他都努力工作。 Whether he (should) be sick or well, he works hard.
- whether 引导的让步状语从句在虚拟语气中可倒装，其方法是：省略whether 和should，
把be 动词提前。
倒装：Be he sick or well, he works hard.

### 总结
- 当我们说出命令、要求、建议、评价等内容是，往往给人一种高人一等的感受。为避免这
个高姿态的感觉，我们可以采用低姿态的虚拟语气句式，去中和句子本身的压迫感。
- 这种用法下，should 通常有三种含义：应该、竟然、以及表达可能性。其中，译为"竟然
"的时候，通常不省略。其他可省略。

## 45.4 虚拟语气4

### 委婉的语气：情态动词的过去式
情态动词的过去式，作为独立的情态动词，语气更委婉。
- can – could
- may – might
- will – would
- shall – should

今天晚上来跟我们聊天好吗？Would you like to have a talk with us this evening?
我可以用一下你的单车吗？ Could I use your bike now?
你本该早点到这里。 You should have got here earlier.

### 虔诚的内心：用may 表示可能性
1. 在表示"无论"的句子中，用may do 表示现在或将来的可能性，用may have done 表示过
去的可能性。
不管他可能去哪儿，我都要跟随他。
I will follow him no matter where he may go.
I will follow him wherever he may go.

不管他干了什么，我们必须尊敬他。
We must respect him no matter what he may have done.
We must respect him whatever he may have done.

2. 在 so that 或 in order that 后用 may/might 表示可能性。
我努力学习以便可以服务大众。 I study hard so that I may serve people.
- 此时，may 常被can 代替
为了别人可以获救，他牺牲了。 He died in order that others might be saved.
- 此时，might 常被could 代替

3. "may+句子"表示祝福。本质是希望may 后面的句子有发生的可能性。
祝你一路顺风！ May you have a good journey!
祝您快乐！ May you be happy!

- 表示祝福时，may 有时可以省略
May God bless you. 愿上帝保佑你。
省略：God bless you.
May you have a good time. 祝你玩得开心。
省略：Have a good time.

## 45.5 简单原则-虚拟语气

### 简单原则-虚拟语气
If you are happy, you will dance. 如果你高兴，你就去跳舞。（if 真实条件句）
时态倒退：If you were happy, you would dance. 如果你高兴，你就去跳舞。
- 虚拟语气：if 虚拟条件句

- 我们最开始学be 动词时，一共学了几种形式？
三种：am / is / are
- 使用简单原则，省去相同内容。if 虚拟条件句中，be 动词统一为：were
If you were happy, you would dance. 如果你高兴，你就去跳舞。
If he were happy, he would dance. 如果他高兴，他就去跳舞。
If I were happy, I would dance. 如果我高兴，我就去跳舞。

Have a good time. 玩得开心。
这是祈使句吗？
全称：May you have a good time.

Happy new year. 新年快乐。
这句话怎么连动词都没有？
全称：May you have a happy new year.
