# 第35章 主语从句

## 35.1 主语从句

### 主语从句的引导词
我们每天都吃饭。 We eat meals every day.
这很重要。 This is important.
- This = We eat meals every day
We eat meals every day is important.

We eat meals every day is important. （错）
这句话谁是主语？是 we？还是 We eat meals every day？
That we eat meals every day is important.
- That 引导陈述句，无意义，但不可省略。

你幸福吗？ Are you happy?
这很难说。 It is hard to say.
- It = Are you happy
Are you happy is hard to say. （错）
You are happy is hard to say. （错）
Whether you are happy is hard to say.

你会游泳吗？ Can you swim?
这很难说。 It is hard to say.
- It = Can you swim
Can you swim is hard to say. （错）
You can swim is hard to say. （错）
Whether you can swim is hard to say.

你喜欢牛肉吗？ Do you like beef?
这很难说。 It is hard to say.
- It = Do you like beef
Do you like beef is hard to say. （错）
You like beef is hard to say. （错）
Whether you like beef is hard to say.
- if/whether 引导一般疑问句，译为是否。if 引导主语从句时不用于句首。

他们一会儿要去哪儿？ Where will they go?
这是个问题。 It is a question.
- It = Where will they go
Where will they go is a question. （错）
Where they will go is a question.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句。

### 语序
- 主语从句的语序：陈述语序（主语 + 动词）
Is he an elephant?
_____ is a question.
Whether … is a question.
Whether he is an elephant is a question.

Does he like elephants?
_____ is a question.
Whether … is a question.
Whether he likes elephants is a question.

Who will the man meet?
_____ is unknown.
Who … is unknown.
Who the man will meet is unknown.

What made him so happy?
_____ is unknown.
What … is unknown.
What made him so happy is unknown.

Whether he likes pigs is unknown.
is unknown whether he likes pigs. （错）
It is unknown whether he likes pigs.

It is unknown whether he likes pigs.
It is unknown if he likes pigs.

Where they will go is a question.
It is a question where they will go.

## 35.2 简单原则-主语从句

### 简单原则-主语从句
1. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
He is a teacher. 他是一个老师。
It is well-known. 这是众所周知的。
He is a teacher it is well-known.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
He is a teacher is well-known. （× 句意混乱）
英语中为了明确主、从句的划分，习惯于给从句加引导词
That he is a teacher is well-known.
- 于是形成了主语从句
- that 引导陈述句，无意义。

2. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
Is she happy? 她开心吗？
It is hard to say. 这很难说。
Is she happy it is hard to say.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
Is she happy is hard to say. （× 句意混乱）
She is happy is hard to say. （× 句意变了）
- 英语中，从句需要引导词
Whether she is happy is hard to say.
- whether 引导一般疑问句。

3. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
What can you do? 你会干什么？
It is hard to say. 这很难说。
What can you do it is hard to say.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
What can you do is hard to say. （× 句意混乱）
What you can do is hard to say.

再看这两句
Who has a hammer? 谁有锤子？
It is hard to say. 这很难说。
Who has a hammer it is hard to say.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
Who has a hammer is hard to say.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句
