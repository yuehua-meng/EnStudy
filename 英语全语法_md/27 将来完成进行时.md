## 27 将来完成进行时

### 一个简单的词组
将一直做某事
will have been + 动词ing （全部人称）
shall have been + 动词ing （第一人称）

将一直吃 will have been eating
将一直跑 will have been running
将一直睡觉 will have been sleeping
将一直唱歌 will have been singing songs
将一直读书 will have been reading books
将一直谈论关于老师的事情 will have been talking about the teacher

明晚之前，我将一直在路上走。
I will have been walking in the street before tomorrow evening.
在爸爸回来之前，你将一直在床下写作业。
You will have been doing homework under the bed before your dad comes back.
我将一直在等录取通知，直到今年年底。
I will have been waiting for an offer until the end of this year.

### 句型转换
他们五点之前将一直睡觉。
They will have been sleeping before five.
否定句：
在五点之前，他们并不会一直睡觉。
They won't have been sleeping before five.
疑问句：
他们五点之前将一直睡觉吗？
Will they have been sleeping before five?
肯定回答：Yes, they will.
否定回答：No, they won't.
