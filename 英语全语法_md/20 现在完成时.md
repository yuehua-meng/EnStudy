# 第20章 现在完成时

## 20.1 现在完成时1

### 一个简单的词组
已经做某事
have + 动词的过去分词
has + 动词的过去分词

- 动词的过去分词变化规则：
1. 一般情况下直接加ed。如：jump – jumped
2. 以e 结尾的，直接加d。如：live – lived
3. 以“辅元辅”结尾，其中元音字母单独发音且处于重音时，双写尾字母加ed。如：stop –
stopped
4. 以“辅音字母 + y”结尾，把y 变成i 加ed。如：study – studied
5. 不规则变化。如：eat – eaten, leave – left

已经停止 have stopped
已经吃 have eaten
已经离开 have left
已经读过书 have read the book
已经看过这场电影 have seen the film
已经丢了作业 have lost the homework

我已经听说这个消息了。 I have heard the news.
他已经喝了10 瓶可乐了。 He has drunk ten bottles of cola.
他们已经去过月球了。 They have been to the moon.
老师已经去了厕所了。 The teacher has gone to the toilet.
我们已经在这里住三个月了。 We have lived here for three months.
王钢蛋自从1873 年就是女孩了。 Wang Gangdan has been a girl since 1873.

自从他离开已经3 年了。
It has been three years since he left.
It is three years since he left.
- 注意：”自从…已经多长时间了”这个句式既可以用现在完成时，也可以用一般现在时。

电影已经开始10 分钟了。
The film has begun for 10 minutes. （错。begin 不能持续10 分钟）
The film has been on for 10 minutes.
她从上个月就已经借了那本书了。
She has borrowed that book since last month. （错。borrow 不能持续1 个月）
She has kept that book since last month.

## 20.2 现在完成时2

### 句型变换
诸葛豆芽已经完成作业了。
Zhuge Douya has already finished his homework.
否定句：
诸葛豆芽还没有完成作业。
Zhuge Douya hasn't finished his homework yet.
疑问句：
诸葛豆芽已经完成作业了吗？
Has Zhuge Douya finished his homework yet?
Yes, he has.
No, he hasn't.

### 何为“已经”？
- 去厕所吧。 Let's go to the toilet.
- 我已经吃过晚饭了。 I have already had dinner.

- 做作业去。 Do your homework.
- 我的手已经丢了。 I have lost my hands.

现在完成时的本质含义：
有话不直说，让别人体会言外之意。

I have heard the news.
He has drunk ten bottles of cola.
They have been to the moon.
The teacher has gone to the toilet.
We have lived here for three months.
Wang Gangdan has been a girl since 1873.
It has been three years since he left.
The film has been on for 10 minutes.
She has kept that book since last month.
