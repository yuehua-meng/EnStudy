# 第36章 表语从句

## 36.1 表语从句

### 什么是表语
主+系+表
I am a teacher. 我是一个老师。
- 跟在连系动词后面的成分叫做表语。
事实是我有很多时间。 The fact is I have a lot of time.
真相是大卫偷了作业。 The truth is David stole the homework.

### 表语从句的引导词
I have a lot of time.
The fact is _____.
The fact is I have a lot of time.
The fact is that I have a lot of time.
- that 引导陈述句，无意义，书面语中不可省略。

好像老师很傻。 It looks as if the teacher is stupid.
那是因为我饿了。 That is because I am hungry.
- 陈述句的引导词还可以是as if, because 等。

Are you an elephant?
The question is _____.
The question is are you an elephant. （错）
The question is whether you are an elephant.

Can you eat the apple?
The question is _____.
The question is can you eat the apple. （错）
The question is whether you can eat the apple.

Do you have dinner every day?
The question is _____.
The question is do you have dinner every day. （错）
The question is whether you have dinner every day.
- whether 引导一般疑问句，译为是否。

What is her name?
The question is _____.
The question is what is her name. （错）
The question is what her name is.

Who will wash the clothes?
The question is _____.
The question is who will wash the clothes.

- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句。

### 表语从句的语序
陈述语序：主语 + 动词
Are you OK?
The question is whether you are OK.

Where are you going to sleep?
The question is where you are going to sleep.

Why do you like elephants?
The question is why you like elephants.

What made me so sad?
The question is what made me so sad.

### 表语从句的时态
- 当主句为现在时，从句可用各种时态。
事实情况是你现在正在吃晚饭。 The fact is that you are eating dinner now.
事实情况是他很快就会回来。 The fact is that he will come back soon.
- 当主句为过去时，从句可用各种过去时态。
事实情况是你正在吃晚饭。 The fact was that you were eating dinner.
事实情况是他很快就会回来。 The fact was that he would come back soon.

## 36.2 简单原则-表语从句

### 简单原则-表语从句
1. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
The fact is it. 事实就是这样。
He is a teacher. 他是一个老师。
The fact is it he is a teacher.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
The fact is he is a teacher.
英语中为了明确主、从句的划分，习惯于给从句加引导词
The fact is that he is a teacher.
- 于是形成了表语从句
- that 引导陈述句，无意义。

2. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
The question is it. 问题就是这个。
Is she happy? 她开心吗？
The question is it is she happy.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
The question is is she happy. （× 标点冲突）
The question is she is happy. （× 句意变了）
- 英语中，从句需要引导词
The question is whether she is happy.
- whether 引导一般疑问句。

3. 把下面两句话加起来会怎样？
The question is it. 问题就是这个。
What is it? 那是什么？
The question is it what is it.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
The question is what is it. （× 标点冲突）
The question is what it is.

再看这两句
The question is it. 问题就是这个。
Who has a hammer? 谁有锤子？
The question is it who has a hammer.
- 使用简单原则，避免重复
The question is who has a hammer.
- 特殊疑问词引导特殊疑问句
